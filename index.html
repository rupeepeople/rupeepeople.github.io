Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions from Families & NRIs

Clear answers on security, portal coverage, statutory boundaries, and refund protection.

1. Do you ever ask for bank passwords, PINs, or take a percentage of recovered money?
Never. RupeePeople operates on two unbreakable rules: (1) We never ask for NetBanking passwords, ATM PINs, or UPI PINs — any official government portal OTP (UDGAM, EPFO, MF Central) is entered directly by you; and (2) We charge only upfront fixed documentation fees (₹998, ₹2,498, or ₹4,998). 100% of the recovered money is credited directly by the financial institution to your verified bank account.
2. How can you trace assets if our family doesn't have account numbers or policy bonds?
Indian statutory registries — including RBI UDGAM (30+ banks), MCA IEPFA (unclaimed shares & dividends), IRDAI Bima Bharosa, and MF Central — allow authorized searches using combinations of the asset holder's Name, PAN, Date of Birth, Father/Spouse Name, and historical city/address variations. Our specialists run structured name-permutation sweeps and compile the exact UDRN/folio references into your Evidence Pack.
3. Where can I download or sign the Client Authorization & Consent Letter?
Our official Consent Form is available online 24×7 at www.rupeepeople.com/consent.html. You can either download the blank A4 PDF (RupeePeople_Client_Authorization_and_Consent_Letter.pdf) to sign on paper, or use our instant browser e-sign tool to generate a SHA-256 verified certificate in 30 seconds.
4. Is RupeePeople a law firm or SEBI-registered Investment Adviser?
No. In strict compliance with the Advocates Act, 1961 and SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013, RupeePeople Private Limited is an asset search technology and administrative documentation support company. We do not provide court litigation, legal representation, or buy/sell stock advice. When court probate or succession certificates are required, our Evidence Pack serves as a ready-made institutional record dossier for your independent advocate.
5. What is your cancellation and refund policy?
If you request cancellation within 24 hours of payment or at any time before our specialists initiate portal verification searches, we issue a 100% refund to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. See our full Cancellation & Refund Policy.
